- **Plum (`CHAR_PLUM_01`)**: Cute small round violet-purple songbird with an oversized head, dark-purple speckled crown and breast feathers, a warm golden-orange belly, a tiny yellow beak, and stubby wings.

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Storyline & Interactive Phonics Script

### Scene 1 — Target Word: `stag` (`/s/ - /t/ - /a/ - /g/`)
- **Visual Composition**: Sunlit woodland clearing framed by oak and pine trees. Clem stands on the left in quiet wonder while Plum perches on a low branch holding one wing up gently ("Shh..."). A tall, friendly brown forest stag with branching golden-tan antlers steps gracefully out from between the trees on the right.
- **Utterances**:
  - `[Narrator]`: "Next, Clem and Plum walked into the cool, shady woods."
  - `[Plum]`: "Shh... look!"
  - `[Narrator]`: "Plum whispered. A tall stag stepped out from the trees."
  - `[Plum]`: "Whoa!"
  - `[Narrator]`: "He stood so proud and still."
  - `[Plum]`: "Look at his big antlers, Clem! Can you sound out stag with me? Sss, tuh, ahh, guh! Stag!"

### Scene 2 — Target Word: `trot` (`/t/ - /r/ - /o/ - /t/`)
- **Visual Composition**: Winding dirt forest trail bordered by ferns and tree trunks. The friendly antlered stag lifts two diagonal hooves in a graceful, bouncy trot along the path while Clem and Plum (perched on a mossy log) watch with big admiring smiles.
- **Utterances**:
  - `[Narrator]`: "Then the stag began to trot down the path. Trot, trot, trot!"
  - `[Clem]`: "He moves so fast and light! Let's sound out trot together! Tuh, rrr, oh, tuh! Trot!"
  - `[Plum]`: "Trot, trot, trot! Look at his bouncy hooves go!"

### Scene 3 — Target Word: `twig` (`/t/ - /w/ - /i/ - /g/`)
- **Visual Composition**: Sunlit forest path. Clem looks down in gentle surprise as his brown boot steps on a small fallen wooden twig that snaps in two with tiny green leaves attached. Plum perches on a low branch to the left with a reassuring smile while the stag looks back calmly in the background right.
- **Utterances**:
  - `[Narrator]`: "Clem took a step to follow, and SNAP! He stepped on a dry twig."
  - `[Clem]`: "Oops! My boot snapped a little brown twig! Tuh, wuh, ih, guh! Twig!"
  - `[Plum]`: "Haha! That was a loud snap, Clem! Good thing the friendly stag did not mind at all!"

### Scene 4 — Target Word: `skin` (`/s/ - /k/ - /i/ - /n/`)
- **Visual Composition**: Sunny forest clearing with smooth gray rocks and fallen leaves. Clem crouches on one knee on the left pointing in fascination while Plum peeks from a mossy stone. On a flat rock on the right, a cute green woodland lizard slips happily past its pale translucent shed skin.
- **Utterances**:
  - `[Narrator]`: "They looked down by a rock and saw something neat."
  - `[Clem]`: "Wow!"
  - `[Narrator]`: "said Clem."
  - `[Clem]`: "A little lizard shed its old skin!"
  - `[Plum]`: "It left its shiny pale skin right on the warm stone! Let's sound out skin! Sss, kuh, ih, nnn! Skin!"

### Scene 5 — Target Word: `flap` (`/f/ - /l/ - /a/ - /p/`)
- **Visual Composition**: Woodland trail surrounded by leafy bushes. A cheerful round woodland robin/pheasant rises up from a green bush in the upper-right with a big joyful flap of both wide-spread feathered wings. Clem raises his hands in playful surprise with a big laughing grin while Plum hovers and laughs in the center.
- **Utterances**:
  - `[Narrator]`: "Suddenly, a bird in the bush gave a big flap of its wings—flap, flap, flap! It made Clem jump, and then they both laughed."
  - `[Clem]`: "Haha! That bird gave such a big flap! Fff, lll, ahh, puh! Flap!"
  - `[Plum]`: "Flap, flap, flap! What an awesome adventure in the woods, Clem!"
