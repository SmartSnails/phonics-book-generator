# Activity Page Composer & PDF Book Builder (`activity-page-composer`)

Composes individual atomic illustrations, handwriting/tracing lines, Word Find SVGs, and full-page story scenes into a cohesive, print-ready **US Letter (`8.5" × 11"`, `612 × 792 pt`) PDF Coloring Book**.

---

## 1. Supported Page Templates

1. **Title / Cover Page**:
   - Book title, subtitle, decorative frame, and featured animal characters.
2. **Theme Section Divider / Intro Header**:
   - Introduces the theme (e.g., *Part 1: Farm Animals*, *Part 2: Jungle Animals*).
3. **Dual-Animal Writing & Coloring Page (`2 Related Animals Per Page`)**:
   - **Upper Half (Animal 1)** & **Lower Half (Animal 2)** enclosed in clean rounded activity cards.
   - Each half contains:
     - **Left/Center**: Large, crisp B&W coloring illustration of the animal (with all grey/background artifacts thresholded to pure `#000000` on `#FFFFFF`).
     - **Right**:
       - Relationship badge (e.g., *Meadow Grazers*, *Jungle Big Cats*).
       - Large bold model word (`COW`, `HORSE`).
       - **Primary Handwriting & Tracing Block**: Top solid line, middle dashed midline, bottom solid baseline, with light traceable letter outlines on Line 1 and blank practice lines on Line 2 so kids can trace and write the animal's name.
4. **Full-Page Story Coloring Pages**:
   - Full-page framed B&W scene illustration (`3:4` portrait inside `8.5" × 11"` page) with story beat header and caption at the bottom.
5. **Theme Word Find Puzzle Page**:
   - Embeds the crisp vector/high-res Word Find puzzle generated by `word-find-generator`.

---

## 2. Mandatory Book Ordering Rule

Within the compiled PDF coloring book, pages are ordered strictly **by theme**, and within each theme:
1. **Individual Animal Pages First** (*2 related animals per page* with handwriting/tracing lines + the theme's *Word Find* activity page).
2. **Full-Page Story Coloring Pages Later** (*Scenes 1, 2, and 3* of that theme's storyline).

---

## 3. Execution Script
- [`scripts/build_coloring_book_pdf.swift`](./scripts/build_coloring_book_pdf.swift): Native macOS CoreGraphics/PDFKit compiler that renders crisp vector typography, primary handwriting lines, thresholded pure B&W animal illustrations, full-page story scenes, and Word Find puzzles into a single multi-page `.pdf` file.
