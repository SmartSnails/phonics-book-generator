# Word Selection & Relatability Rubric

## 1. Age-Band Vocabulary Standards
- **Ages 3–4 (Preschool)**: 3–4 letters, 1 syllable, everyday household/barnyard/zoo animals and objects (`cat`, `dog`, `pig`, `cow`, `sun`, `hat`).
- **Ages 4–6 (Pre-K to Kindergarten)**: 3–6 letters, 1–2 syllables, high-imagery nouns and animals with distinctive silhouettes (`cow`, `horse`, `sheep`, `goat`, `duck`, `tiger`, `zebra`, `monkey`, `snake`, `frog`, `panda`, `toucan`, `sloth`, `hippo`, `lion`).
- **Ages 6–8 (Early Elementary)**: 5–9 letters, multi-syllable words, habitats, and specific species (`rooster`, `chameleon`, `crocodile`, `flamingo`, `tractor`).

## 2. Silhouette & B&W Line-Art Distinctiveness
Because coloring book pages are rendered in pure black-and-white line art without color cues:
- Every chosen animal or object must have an **unmistakable silhouette or pattern in B&W** (e.g., `zebra` has bold stripes; `tiger` has flame stripes + feline whiskers; `lion` has a fluffy mane; `monkey` has a curled tail and big ears; `snake` has a coiled body and forked tongue; `cow` has rounded spots, horns, and a bell/udder; `sheep` has a scalloped cloud-like wool coat).
- Avoid pairing two animals on the same page whose B&W outlines look nearly identical unless their distinguishing features are explicitly emphasized in the subject prompt.

## 3. Related-Pair Grouping Rules (2 Per Page)
When generating words for dual-animal coloring + handwriting pages, group words into pairs that share a micro-habitat or relationship:
- **Farm Pairs**:
  - *Pasture Grazers*: `COW` + `HORSE`
  - *Woolly & Horned Friends*: `SHEEP` + `GOAT`
  - *Barnyard & Pond Pals*: `PIG` + `DUCK`
- **Jungle Pairs**:
  - *Big Cats & Savanna/Jungle Royals*: `LION` + `TIGER`
  - *Treetop Canopy Climbers*: `MONKEY` + `SLOTH` (or `PANDA` / `KOALA`)
  - *Striped & Scaled Explorers*: `ZEBRA` + `SNAKE` (or `FROG` / `TOUCAN`)
